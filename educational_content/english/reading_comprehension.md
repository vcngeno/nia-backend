# Reading Comprehension Strategies

## Finding the Main Idea

The main idea is what the text is mostly about. It's the most important point the author wants you to understand.

**Strategy**: Ask yourself "What is this mostly about?" after reading each paragraph.

## Supporting Details

Supporting details are facts, examples, and descriptions that explain or prove the main idea.

## Making Inferences

An inference is a conclusion you reach based on evidence in the text and what you already know.

**Example**: If a story says "Sarah grabbed her umbrella and raincoat," you can infer it's raining or about to rain.

## Reading Strategies

1. **Preview**: Look at titles, headings, and pictures before reading
2. **Ask questions**: What do I want to know? What is this about?
3. **Visualize**: Create mental pictures of what you're reading
4. **Summarize**: Retell the main points in your own words
