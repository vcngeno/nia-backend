# United States Geography

## US Regions

The United States is divided into five main regions:

1. **Northeast**: Known for historical cities, dense population, four distinct seasons
2. **Southeast**: Warm climate, coastal areas, agricultural importance
3. **Midwest**: "America's Heartland," major farming region, Great Lakes
4. **Southwest**: Desert climate, Spanish influence, growing population
5. **West**: Mountain ranges, Pacific coast, diverse climates

## Major Landforms

- **Rocky Mountains**: Major mountain range in western US
- **Appalachian Mountains**: Older mountain range in eastern US
- **Great Plains**: Flat grasslands in central US, important for farming
- **Mississippi River**: Major river system flowing from Minnesota to Gulf of Mexico

## Using Maps

**Map elements**:
- Title: tells what the map shows
- Legend/Key: explains symbols and colors
- Compass Rose: shows directions (N, S, E, W)
- Scale: shows distance on the map
