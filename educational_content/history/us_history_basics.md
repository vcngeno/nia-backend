# Early American History

## Colonial America (1600s-1700s)

European settlers established 13 colonies along the Atlantic coast. The colonies were divided into three regions:
- **New England Colonies**: Fishing, shipbuilding, trade
- **Middle Colonies**: Farming, diverse population
- **Southern Colonies**: Large plantations, tobacco and cotton farming

## American Revolution (1775-1783)

The 13 colonies fought for independence from British rule.

**Key Events**:
- 1775: Battles of Lexington and Concord (first battles)
- 1776: Declaration of Independence signed
- 1781: Battle of Yorktown (final major battle)
- 1783: Treaty of Paris (Britain recognizes US independence)

## The Constitution (1787)

The US Constitution established our system of government with three branches:
- **Legislative Branch** (Congress): Makes laws
- **Executive Branch** (President): Enforces laws
- **Judicial Branch** (Courts): Interprets laws

This system of "checks and balances" ensures no single branch becomes too powerful.
