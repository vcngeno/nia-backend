# Life Science: Living Things and Ecosystems

## Characteristics of Living Things

All living things:
- Need food, water, and air
- Grow and develop
- Reproduce (make more of their kind)
- Respond to their environment
- Are made of cells

## Ecosystems

An ecosystem includes all living things (plants, animals) and non-living things (water, soil, sunlight) in an area.

## Food Chains

A food chain shows how energy moves from one living thing to another.

**Example**:
Sun → Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Hawk

- **Producers** (plants) make their own food using sunlight
- **Consumers** (animals) eat other living things
- **Decomposers** break down dead plants and animals
