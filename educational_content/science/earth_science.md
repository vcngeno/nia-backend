# Earth Science: Weather and Climate

## The Water Cycle

The water cycle is the continuous movement of water on, above, and below Earth's surface.

**Steps**:
1. **Evaporation**: Water from oceans, lakes, and rivers turns into water vapor
2. **Condensation**: Water vapor cools and forms clouds
3. **Precipitation**: Water falls as rain, snow, sleet, or hail
4. **Collection**: Water collects in bodies of water and the cycle repeats

## Weather vs Climate

**Weather** is the day-to-day conditions in the atmosphere (temperature, precipitation, wind).

**Climate** is the average weather patterns in an area over a long period (30+ years).

## The Atmosphere

Earth's atmosphere is made of layers of gases that surround our planet. It protects us from harmful solar radiation and helps regulate temperature.
